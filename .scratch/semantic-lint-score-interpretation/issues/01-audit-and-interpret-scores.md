# Audit and interpret semantic scores

Category: enhancement
Status: ready-for-agent
Sources: [GitHub #11](https://github.com/andrueandersoncs/better-typescript/issues/11), [GitHub #12](https://github.com/andrueandersoncs/better-typescript/issues/12)

## Established

The reports describe score variation on unchanged source. Accept those observations; the goal is auditable interpretation, not deterministic subjective judgments or a new 0.50 gate.

Current `FindingReport` contains source path, returned model, violation threshold, findings, and token usage. Actual CLI smoke confirmed those fields. It carries no source/policy identity or evaluation-version identity. The current documentation gives classification thresholds, but no repeated-run calibration guidance.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Make semantic score comparisons auditable without traces or unsupported calibration claims.

**Current behavior:** Reports contain paths, returned model, threshold, findings, and usage, but no source/policy/evaluator identity.

**Desired behavior:** Reports identify the complete source, effective policies/settings, requested and returned model identities, and evaluator contract behind a comparison. Use deterministic content fingerprints and versioned metadata, not stored provider requests/responses. Documentation explains the distinct relevance, applicability, and violation judgments and the limits of repeated-run comparisons.

**Key interfaces:**
- `FindingReport`: versioned audit metadata for source and effective evaluation settings.
- `Rule`/`Finding`: identify the effective policy and its verbatim content fingerprint.
- `evaluationRequest`/`evaluationResponse`: report requested/returned model identifiers actually available; do not invent provider internals.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Source identity includes its project-relative path and complete source-content fingerprint; each effective policy has a path/content identity.
- [ ] Metadata records effective thresholds, requested/returned model identifiers, and a versioned evaluator identity covering prompts, routing, classification, and explanation semantics.
- [ ] Equal effective inputs have equal fingerprints regardless of report formatting or incidental collection order. Changed source, path, policy text, effective settings, or evaluator semantics is detectable.
- [ ] Guidance distinguishes unchanged source from identical final evidence: candidate ranges may differ between runs. Reported model identifiers do not certify unchanged provider weights or deterministic predictions.
- [ ] Documentation retains the current thresholds and explains that above-cutoff pairs are not confirmed defects. No numerical noise tolerance, confidence interval, or calibrated-accuracy claim is introduced.
- [ ] Reports contain no request/response traces, credentials, or newly retained source payloads. Narrow identity/comparison coverage, actual CLI smoke, and `./scripts/check.sh` pass.

**Out of scope:** Request replay, trace storage, measured calibration studies, new caching/retries/telemetry, threshold changes, deterministic-score guarantees, or refactoring suggestions.
