# Informally Verify Shirky principle

Read the law's exact wording and context in [`../reference.md`](../reference.md), then follow the shared [`informal-verification` method](../informal-verification.md).

## Investigation

> Identify how an institution benefits from the problem it claims to solve. Trace proposals for durable fixes and what happened to them. Compare incentive-based explanations with cases where the institution reduced or eliminated its own role.
