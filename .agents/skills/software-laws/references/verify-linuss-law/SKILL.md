# Informally Verify Linus's Law

Read the law's exact wording and context in [`../reference.md`](../reference.md), then follow the shared [`informal-verification` method](../informal-verification.md).

## Investigation

> Compare reviewer participation with time to diagnose and repair bugs. Control for bug severity and complexity. Identify when an additional reviewer supplied the key insight and when many reviewers still failed.
