# Informally Verify Kerchkhoff's principle

Read the law's exact wording and context in [`../reference.md`](../reference.md), then follow the shared [`informal-verification` method](../informal-verification.md).

## Investigation

> Inventory what an attacker can learn and which secrets remain protected. Evaluate the system while assuming its source and design are public. Identify protections that depend on hiding the design rather than protecting keys or data.
