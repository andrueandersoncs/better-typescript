# Informally Verify Jevons Paradox

Read the law's exact wording and context in [`../reference.md`](../reference.md), then follow the shared [`informal-verification` method](../informal-verification.md).

## Investigation

> Find a change that reduced the unit cost of compute, storage, network use, or development effort. Compare unit cost and total consumption before and after while accounting for workload growth.
