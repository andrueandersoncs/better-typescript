# AI Opportunity Assessment

Produce a decision document the client can use independently.

## Inputs

Use the available discovery transcript, business context, desired outcomes, and constraints. Treat the client's own words and supplied numbers as the primary evidence.

## Method

1. State the client's desired outcomes and current situation in their language.
2. Extract the pains, bottlenecks, costs, and risks supported by the discovery material.
3. Identify possible interventions and classify each by its primary business outcome:
   - **Effectiveness:** increases revenue or improves decisions.
   - **Efficiency:** saves time or reduces cost.
   - **Quality:** improves the product or customer experience.
4. Recommend the simplest effective intervention. Distinguish AI from conventional automation or process changes, and use AI only when it adds material value.
5. Prioritize the opportunities by expected value, strength of evidence, and implementation effort.
6. Quantify projected outcomes from supplied facts. Label assumptions, ranges, and unknowns clearly.
7. Turn the priorities into the smallest practical implementation sequence. Give each phase an outcome and a way to verify it.

## Output

Return one client-ready assessment with:

- An executive brief suitable for decision-makers.
- The desired outcomes and supporting evidence.
- A prioritized opportunity scorecard showing outcome, evidence, intervention type, expected value, and effort.
- A phased implementation roadmap.
- Projected outcomes, assumptions, and measurements.

Keep collected revenue, signed contracts, projected savings, and pipeline value separate.
