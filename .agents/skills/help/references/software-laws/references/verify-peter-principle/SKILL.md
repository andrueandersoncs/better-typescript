# Informally Verify Peter Principle

Read the law's exact wording and context in [`../reference.md`](../reference.md), then follow the shared [`informal-verification` method](../informal-verification.md).

## Investigation

> Compare role-specific performance before and after promotions. Record how required skills changed and whether weaker performance stopped further promotion. Look for people who improved through training or remained effective after repeated promotions.
