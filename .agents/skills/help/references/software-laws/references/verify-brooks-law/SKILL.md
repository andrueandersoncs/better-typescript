# Informally Verify Brooks's Law

Read the law's exact wording and context in [`../reference.md`](../reference.md), then follow the shared [`informal-verification` method](../informal-verification.md).

## Investigation

> Find a project that was already late when people were added. Compare onboarding work, communication load, throughput, and forecast slippage before and after they joined. Look for other causes of delay.
