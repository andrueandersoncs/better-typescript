# Shared marketing context

Read this before a marketing workflow. Reuse the request and existing project artifacts; gather only information that can change the requested decision or deliverable. A missing marketing brief is not a reason to start a separate intake project.

## Reuse product truth

[Product management](../../product-management/SKILL.md) owns durable product facts, customer evidence, positioning, and proposed product behavior. Read the relevant existing product record, research, site, or brief. If an upstream `.agents/product-marketing.md` or another established brief is already supplied or discoverable, use it as evidence without copying or migrating it automatically. Do not create a parallel marketing-context file or require a particular filename.

Extract only what the task needs:

- Product and current capabilities, limits, pricing, and availability; distinguish approved changes from shipped behavior.
- Audience and buying situation: user versus buyer, problem, job, alternatives including doing nothing, and switching obstacles.
- Positioning: relevant difference, why the customer values it, objections, and who is not a fit.
- Evidence: source-backed customer language, research, measured results, and permission to use names, logos, or testimonials.
- Requested outcome: intended action, traffic or triggering event, current measurement, constraints, and the user's voice or examples.

Prefer the authoritative product record for implemented behavior. If records and public copy conflict, identify the discrepancy and resolve the consequential fact rather than silently choosing the more persuasive version. Return new evidence or proposals with a link to the existing record; do not promote them to product truth without the owning decision.

## Keep claims supported

Keep observations, attributed customer statements, interpretations, and hypotheses distinct. Preserve source URLs or artifact locations, dates, population, and denominators when available. Multiple copies of one review are one source. A customer quote establishes what that customer said, not a general performance result.

Never invent customer counts, testimonials, integrations, savings, trial terms, compliance status, or measured lift. Omit unsupported proof from finished copy and name consequential gaps separately. Do not leave fake examples or placeholders where the user asked for publishable text. Ask a targeted question only when the missing fact prevents a correct deliverable; otherwise finish the supported portion without claiming completeness beyond it.

Treat marketing benchmarks, sampling cutoffs, and platform tactics as context-dependent hypotheses, not guarantees. Link current primary documentation when a technical or platform rule changes the recommendation. Tie success to the user's actual outcome rather than clicks, opens, or apparent engagement alone.

## Keep ownership and authority narrow

The selected workflow owns its requested artifact. Use [deslop](../../deslop/SKILL.md) for bounded prose cleanup, [predictive planning](../../predictive-planning/SKILL.md) when a consequential proposal needs forecasts and response rules, and [software craft](../../software-craft/SKILL.md) for approved implementation. Pass the evidence and preserve the original deliverable; do not reroute it through every related skill.

A drafting, research, or planning request does not authorize publishing, sending messages, enrolling recipients, changing prices or billing, buying tools, or uploading customer data. Use only available, authorized capabilities. Return a reviewable artifact when execution requires separate approval or access.
